THE PRODUCT STORY

Work becomes evidence.
Evidence becomes knowledge.
Knowledge returns as answers.

Engineering work creates scattered evidence. Context Recall captures it without replacing it, lets knowledge settle through explicit stages, and returns answers that stay linked to their source - so the next engineer can continue instead of re-investigating.
